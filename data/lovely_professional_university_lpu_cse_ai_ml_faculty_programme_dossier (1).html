Tuition Fee Structure, LPUNEST Cut-Offs & Phased Scholarship Decay

The base tuition fee for B.Tech. (CSE - Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) is ₹2,00,000 per semester (₹16,00,000 across 8 semesters before scholarship), plus an examination fee of ₹5,000 per semester and a one-time uniform fee of ₹4,000. For international applicants, the listed fee for B.Tech. CSE (Generative AI) is $4,500 per semester (LPU International) and ₹1,80,000–₹2,00,000 per semester for regional international tiers (AI & Data Engg International).

LPU awards scholarships based on LPUNEST scores, 10th/12th aggregate marks, or JEE (Main) percentiles. Crucially, the scholarship percentage varies across five admission phases—peaking at 60% off (₹80,000 net tuition/semester) during the Till 2nd June window and stepping down to 45% off (₹1,10,000 net tuition/semester) from 1st August onwards for Category A+ qualifiers. National Rank Holders and Board Toppers under LPU RISE receive a 100% waiver on both programme fees and 4-seater non-AC hostel accommodation.

Eligibility, LPUNEST 2027 Timeline & Residential Cost Matrix (2026–2027)

Admission to B.Tech. (CSE - AI & ML) requires passing 10+2 with 60% aggregate marks (calculated across the best five subjects including mandatory Physics, Mathematics, and English) plus qualifying LPUNEST, CUET (UG) (minimum 225 marks across English, Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry), or JEE (Main) (Eligibility Criteria). A 10% aggregate relaxation applies to applicants from North-East states and Sikkim, and a 5% relaxation applies to Defence personnel dependents and wards of Kashmiri migrants.
