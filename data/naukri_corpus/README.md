# CampusConnect Naukri & Career Intelligence Corpus

This directory contains curated job postings, LPU curriculum skill mappings, instruction fine-tuning datasets, and evaluation benchmarks for the CampusConnect Student Career Intelligence Engine.

## Components
- `lpu_skill_taxonomy_mapping.json`: Mapping of LPU engineering courses to industry tech skills.
- `job_postings_tech_punjab_delhi.jsonl`: Normalized job descriptions from Naukri.com, Internshala, and campus drives.
- `fine_tuning_instructions.jsonl`: Instruction-tuning dataset for student opportunity matching.
- `validation_benchmark_dataset.jsonl`: Zero-shot and few-shot evaluation benchmark.
- `training_metrics_log.json`: LoRA / QLoRA fine-tuning training loss and evaluation metrics.
